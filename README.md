## What I'm building

### [AudienceMD](https://audiencemd.ai/) · [`AUDIENCE.md`](https://github.com/KikoPalomares/audience.md)

**AudienceMD** is an open Markdown-based standard for defining who a product, project, campaign, or piece of content is for.

It gives people and AI agents a clear, versionable source of truth about an audience before writing copy, designing flows, prioritising features, or making product decisions.

- Website: [audiencemd.ai](https://audiencemd.ai/)
- Repository: [`KikoPalomares/audience.md`](https://github.com/KikoPalomares/audience.md)
- CLI: [`@audiencemd/cli`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@audiencemd/cli)
